FAQ

Questions we often hear.

How do we get started?

Email us a short brief. We reply to arrange a conversation about your goals, users and constraints, then propose a practical plan.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A focused website is much quicker than a SaaS platform. We give a realistic timeline once we understand what you need.

How is pricing decided?

We scope each project individually and share a clear estimate before work begins, based on the features, complexity and engagement model you choose.

Do you work with early-stage ideas?

Yes. Our technology consulting helps shape an early idea into a delivery plan, and we can start with a small first release.

Will we own the code and designs?

Ownership terms are agreed in writing at the start of each engagement, so you know exactly what you receive.

Do you support products after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance, monitoring and improvements, or a clean handover to your own team.
